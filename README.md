# MentorMatchr

MentorMatchr is a web-based platform designed to streamline the process of assigning faculty mentors to teams during college hackathons. The system maintains structured information about faculty members, including their areas of expertise, domains, and relevant technical keywords, along with hackathon problem statements and their associated domains and keywords.

When a team selects a problem statement, MentorMatchr analyzes its domain and keywords and compares them with the expertise stored in the faculty database to identify suitable mentor candidates. The platform provides hackathon coordinators with a centralized system to manage faculty, problem statements, teams, and mentor assignments. By replacing a largely manual selection process with a structured, database-driven matching approach, MentorMatchr aims to make mentor allocation more efficient, organized, and easier to manage.
